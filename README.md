# Swift : Are you drunk enough ?

## Goal of the App

- The only purpose of this app is the fun. I always wanted to develop my own app, and play with my friends on it.

# Objectives

Thoses objectives will maybe change depending on the complexity of the projet.

## Games

- 3 differents games :
    1. Driving license exam questions
    2. Famous questions pour un champion questions
    3. General culture questions

- Playable online with your friends.
- Higher your alcohol level is, higher your multiplicator is !

## Home

- Calculate your alcohol rate
- Mini database containing differents types of alcohol to calculate the alcohol rate

## Profile

- Profile page :
    1. Picture is changable
    2. Notification (activate/desactivate)
    3. Sign out button

- Create a login/register/signout

## Statistic

- Shows when you'll be able to drive again
- Change your weight or your size (for alcohol rate calcule)

## Widget

- Widget with your current alcohol rate
